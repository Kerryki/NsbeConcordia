WHO ARE WE

[Photo of members]

NSBE Concordia is the Concordia University chapter of the National Society of Black Engineers. We are a community of more than 100 engineering students who push each other to excel academically, grow professionally and give back to the community around us. Through study sessions, industry events, mentorship and celebrations of Black excellence, we make sure no member navigates engineering school alone.
