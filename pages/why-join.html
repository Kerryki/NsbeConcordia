Why Join NSBE?

NSBE’s mission is to increase the number of culturally responsible Black engineers who excel academically, succeed professionally and positively impact the community.

At Concordia, our chapter brings that mission home: a community of more than 100 engineering students who study together, grow their careers together, and show up for one another from first year to graduation.

WHAT WE OFFER

Academic Excellence

Study groups, tutoring support, and access to a community that understands the pressures of engineering school.

Professional Development

Resume workshops, mock interviews, industry panels, and connections to top employers actively seeking diverse engineers.

Community & Belonging

A space where you can show up fully as yourself: celebrated, challenged, and supported by people who get it.

Big NSBE Access

Annual access to the National NSBE conference: thousands of engineers, hundreds of recruiters, and career-defining moments.
